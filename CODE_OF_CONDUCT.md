# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Aura a respectful, welcoming, and collaborative experience for everyone.

Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to interact professionally and constructively, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or perspective.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and constructive.
- Giving and receiving feedback professionally.
- Focusing discussions on ideas rather than individuals.
- Helping improve the project through thoughtful collaboration.
- Respecting differing technical opinions.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples include:

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to Aura.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any behavior that makes participation unsafe or hostile.

---

## Project Philosophy

Aura values thoughtful engineering over unnecessary conflict.

Disagreements about architecture, implementation, or design are expected.

They should always be resolved through evidence, reasoning, documentation, and respectful discussion.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from the project.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers privately.

Reports will be handled respectfully and, where possible, confidentially.

---
